🏙️ Smart Cities & Infrastructure

Adaptive Street Lighting & Traffic Optimization

Integrates ambient lux sensors, acoustic vehicle counters, and radar motion sensors with edge gateways to dynamically control roadway illumination and predict congestion.

Deployment Architecture
  • Sensing: PIR motion detector, BH1750 ambient lux sensor, microwave radar.
  • Edge Node: ESP32 with LoRaWAN transceiver; local dimming control with 50ms latency.
  • AI Model: Decision tree + rolling 15-minute traffic forecaster running on zonal edge server.
  • Impact: 42% electrical energy reduction; municipal safety compliance maintained.
💡 Referenced in: National Tsing Hua University Smart City Testbed & OECD.AI Fire Prevention.
🏥 Healthcare & Wearables

Continuous Patient Biometrics & Athlete Recovery

Combines PPG photoplethysmography, 6-axis IMU, and galvanic skin response to infer physical fatigue, cardiac anomalies, and fall events in real time.

Deployment Architecture
  • Sensing: MAX30102 (SpO2/HR), MPU-6050 (6-axis gyro/accel), skin conductance.
  • Edge Node: Nordic nRF52840 BLE SoC running quantized TensorFlow Lite Micro 1D-CNN.
  • AI Model: 1D Convolutional Neural Network for arrhythmia and stride irregularity detection.
  • Privacy & Security: On-device feature extraction; raw PPG stays on wearable, only encrypted inferences transmitted.
💡 Referenced in: Univ. of Verona Athlete Health Framework & Infosys Healthcare Diagnostics.
🌾 Precision Agriculture & Smart Farming

Soil Moisture Closed-Loop Autonomous Irrigation

Battery-powered soil capacitive probes across remote crop acreage communicating via LoRaWAN to an edge irrigation controller with evapotranspiration forecasting.

Deployment Architecture
  • Sensing: Capacitive soil moisture at 15cm & 45cm depths, ambient temp/humidity, solar irradiance.
  • Edge Gateway: Raspberry Pi 4 edge controller with LoRa concentrator hat.
  • AI Model: Penman-Monteith evapotranspiration regression + XGBoost valve duty-cycle optimizer.
  • Water Conservation: 34% reduction in irrigation volume; prevented soil over-saturation and root rot.
💡 Referenced in: Journal of Network & Computer Applications (JNCA 2024) & CRC Press AIoT Guide.
⚙️ Industry 4.0 & Predictive Maintenance

Rotating Machinery Vibration Anomaly Detection

Industrial tri-axial piezoelectric accelerometers sampled at 10 kHz; on-sensor Fast Fourier Transform (FFT) detects bearing fluting and unbalance before catastrophic failure.

Deployment Architecture
  • Sensing: ADXL355 3-axis accelerometer, PT100 temperature probe, hall effect RPM sensor.
  • Edge Processor: STM32H7 / ESP32-S3 computing 1024-point real-time FFT spectral energy bins.
  • AI Model: Isolation Forest + Autoencoder reconstruction error threshold for novelty detection.
  • Outcome: 120 hours mean early warning before motor bearing seizure; 0 unscheduled line stops.
💡 Referenced in: Research Square Industrial Edge-Cloud & IoT Analytics Market Report.
⚡ Smart Energy & Microgrids

Microgrid Load Balancing & Solar Peak Shaving

Sub-metering current transformers (CT) and solar inverter telemetry fed into edge reinforcement learning agents to optimize battery charging and grid dispatch.

Deployment Architecture
  • Sensing: Non-invasive split-core CT clamps, MODBUS RS-485 solar generation meters.
  • Edge Gateway: Industrial DIN-rail Linux edge gateway executing local MODBUS polling.
  • AI Model: LSTM 24-hour solar insolation & building load forecast; Q-learning battery scheduling.
  • ROI: 28% peak demand charge reduction; 99.98% microgrid uptime during grid drops.
💡 Referenced in: Mendeley Data CBAI Autonomous Agents & FRAI Foresight to 2050.
🛰️ Aerospace & Satellite AIoT

LEO Constellation Ground Station Scheduling

Autonomous onboard telemetry classification on Low Earth Orbit (LEO) satellites dynamically schedules downlink slots with distributed ground stations.

Deployment Architecture
  • Sensing: Radiation monitors, solar panel thermal telemetry, star tracker attitude metrics.
  • Edge Node: Radiation-hardened FPGA / neuromorphic coprocessor onboard satellite.
  • AI Model: Spiking Neural Network (SNN) event classifier prioritizing high-value downlink packets.
  • Advantage: Mitigates limited 8-minute ground pass windows by eliminating routine telemetry overhead.
💡 Referenced in: Physical Communication Vol. 74 (2026) & IEEE JIOT Neuromorphic MLOps.
